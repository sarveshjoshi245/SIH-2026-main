📑 उद्योग प्रदूषण श्रेणी वर्गीकरण (Industry Classification - Red/Orange/Green)

पर्यावरण प्रदूषण निर्देशांकानुसार उद्योगाचे वर्गीकरण व आवश्यक संमती कालावधी.

🔴 RED CATEGORY

Heavy Pollution Index (> 60). Detailed EIA clearance required. Validity: 5 Years.

🟠 ORANGE CATEGORY

Moderate Pollution Index (41-59). Standard CTE/CTO consent. Validity: 10 Years.

🟢 GREEN CATEGORY

Low Pollution Index (21-40). Fast-track single-window CTE clearance. Validity: 15 Years.

⚪ WHITE CATEGORY

Non-polluting (< 20). Exempted from CTE/CTO, simple intimation only.
