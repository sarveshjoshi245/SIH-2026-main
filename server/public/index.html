One application. Every department.

Samanvay puts approvals from several government departments into one application. Land Revenue, Electricity and Pollution Control are live on the platform today. You fill in your project details once, and each department that needs them gets them.

Connected government departments

Samanvay asks these departments for your records. Each one still makes its own decision.

Land Revenue Department

7/12 land records

Keeps the 7/12 land records. It tells Samanvay who owns the land, whether the mutation entry is approved, and whether there is a loan, claim or court case on it.

Electricity (MSEDCL)

New power connections

Handles new power connections. It tells Samanvay whether your connection application is approved, whether the connection is ready, and whether any bills are unpaid.

Pollution Control (MPCB)

Permission to set up

Gives permission to set up (Consent to Establish). It tells Samanvay whether your consent is approved and whether your unit meets pollution rules. It is asked only after Land and Electricity have approved.

You still sign, pay fees and make the final submission on each department’s official website.

How Samanvay works

From one application to a result from each connected department.

Step 1 You answer 10 questions

After you log in, you answer 10 questions about your project, once: type of project, industry, district, land needed and power load.

Step 2 We work out what you need

From your answers, Samanvay lists the approvals your project needs and the order they are checked in. Your department reference numbers are asked for only at the end, just before your details are sent for checking.

Step 3 Stage 1: Land + Electricity

Your details go to the Land Revenue Department and MSEDCL at the same time. Samanvay checks your 7/12 land record and your electricity connection application, including that the PAN on each record matches yours.

Step 4 Stage 2: Pollution Control Board

The Pollution Control Board check starts only after both Land and Electricity have approved, because it needs those first. If either is not approved yet, the Pollution check waits.

Step 5 Your overall result

You see one overall result — “All approved”, “Waiting for departments” or “Action needed from you” — with a line for each department saying what its records show. Then you finish on each department’s website.

Help and frequently asked questions

Do I fill in a separate form for each department?
No. You answer the questions once and Samanvay sends your details to each department that needs them. You still sign, pay fees and make the final submission on each department’s official website.
In what order are the departments checked?
Land and Electricity are checked at the same time. The Pollution Control Board check starts only after both have approved.
What do I need to log in?
The Aadhaar or PAN number you registered with. We send a one-time password (OTP) to your registered mobile number.
How do I track my application?
Log in and open “Track my application” on your dashboard.
What happens if a department rejects my application?
Your result shows “Action needed from you”, and each department’s line shows what its records say — for example, that an application was turned down. Where the records give a reason, such as unpaid electricity bills, a claim on the land or a pollution-rule problem, that is shown too; otherwise you are asked to contact that department. Once it is sorted out with the department, send your details for checking again from your dashboard.
What if I don’t have all my reference numbers yet?
You can start without them. The 10 questions and the list of approvals you need don’t ask for reference numbers — they are only asked for at the last step, just before you send your details for checking. Your answers are not saved if you leave the page, so keep it open or be ready to answer the questions again.
Is my information shared with anyone else?
Your details are sent only to the departments Samanvay checks for your application — Land Revenue, Electricity and Pollution Control — and only while your organisation’s consent is on record. Every time your details are shared, it is recorded in an audit log, with your PAN partly hidden.
What happens if a department’s system is temporarily unavailable?
Samanvay tries that department’s system again a couple of times straight away. If it still doesn’t answer, the checks that don’t depend on it still finish, and that department is shown as “Not checked” with the reason. Send your details for checking again a little later. If a department’s system keeps failing, Samanvay pauses calls to it for about 30 seconds so it can recover.
Can I stop sharing my information with a department after I’ve applied?
This can’t be done from Samanvay yet. If you want to withdraw your consent for a department, call the free helpline 1800-120-8040 or email samanvay-support@gov.in.
